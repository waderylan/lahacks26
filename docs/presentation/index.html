Jev is a System One model

A fast decision model. It reads a record and answers a question about it.

Input

  • A question in plain English.
  • Up to 20 records per request: vendor, description, memo, amount, dates.

Output, per record

  • Match, Not enough information, or No match.
  • A confidence score, P(match), from 0 to 1.

Called server-side through Vercel AI Gateway. About 611 ms per batch of 20.

Rules

A rule is an Explore search that acts on the transactions it matches.

A rule has

  • Conditions: English sentences or standard filters, joined with AND, OR and NOT.
  • Actions for each result: Match, Not enough information, No match.
  • A model: Jev or Qwen.

Actions

  • Flag the transaction.
  • Assign it to someone.
  • Create a task with an owner and due date.
  • Draft a message. Jev picks the recipient.

Preview first, then Run, or turn on Keep checking for new and changed records.

Walkthrough

Messages wait for the sender

Jev picks the recipient. Gemini writes the draft. Nothing sends until the sender approves.

Ask your books

A chat panel on every page. Type or speak a question about the company's books.

  • Jev reads what the question wants: the kind of answer, the scope and the period.
  • Answers can preview an action on the matching transactions: flag, assign, task or message.
  • Questions that ask for a graph put the chart on the Dashboard.
  • Conversation history is saved in SQLite.

Graphs from vague prompts

Jev reads what a vague question asks for. The chart follows from its answer.

"graph where our money is going"

"plot expenses by month"

The first question names no grouping. Jev reads it as a vendor question, so the chart groups spend by vendor. Group by, measure and chart type stay editable.
